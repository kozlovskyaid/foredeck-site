What this is

Foredeck is a read-and-control client for Kubernetes clusters. Andrew Kozlowskiy licenses it to you for personal or business use on Apple devices you own or control, on the terms below.

You are responsible for what you do to your clusters

Foredeck can change and delete objects in a cluster when the credential you supply allows it. Those actions take effect immediately and cannot be undone from the app: a deleted pod is deleted, a workload scaled to zero stops serving. You are responsible for the credentials you add, for the permissions they carry, and for the consequences of the actions you take with them. If you want a credential that can only read, bind its ServiceAccount to the view role; Foredeck's read-only switch is a convenience on top of that, not a substitute for it.

Accuracy

Foredeck shows what the API server tells it, interpreted for a small screen. Interpretations can be wrong, a connection can drop while a list looks current, and a cluster can change between one screen and the next. Use it as an instrument, not as the only source of truth for an irreversible decision. It is provided as an operational tool and not as a guarantee about the state of your systems.
